Title: From bailout to backstop: what drives Portugal’s 10-year sovereign spread over Germany, 1999-2026?

Research question: Which global, monetary and domestic fiscal and macroeconomic factors are associated with changes in the spread between Portuguese and German 10-year government bond yields, and did their influence change across crisis and policy regimes?

Data: Monthly 10-year government bond yields for Portugal and Germany (OECD, via FRED), the VIX index and the ECB deposit facility rate (FRED), quarterly government debt-to-GDP and HICP inflation (Eurostat), and unemployment rates (OECD, via FRED), for January 1999 to mid-2026. As an extension, we would add Spain, Italy, Greece and Ireland.

Methods: Exploratory analysis by regime; multiple regression in first differences with tests for multicollinearity, heteroscedasticity, autocorrelation (Newey-West standard errors) and specification, including a Chow test around 2012; unit-root tests, decomposition and ARIMA modelling and forecasting of the spread, compared with a random walk.
